🌿 ElderPath - TECHNATION Canada Hackathon Project

ElderPath is a privacy-first AI navigator that uses local DeepSeek-R1 and Nomic Embeddings to provide Indigenous families with instant, secure guidance on health rights and benefits. By combining local RAG with Claude 3.5 and pgvector, the platform bridges administrative gaps in Jordan’s Principle and NIHB coverage with zero-hallucination accuracy.

Technical Stack

  • LLMs: Local DeepSeek-R1 (1.5B/7B) via Ollama and Claude 3.5 Sonnet.
  • Search & Retrieval: Nomic-Embed-Text with PostgreSQL (pgvector) for hybrid fuzzy-semantic search.
  • Infrastructure: Streamlit UI with a custom Python-based Multi-Agent Router for intent-based task delegation.
